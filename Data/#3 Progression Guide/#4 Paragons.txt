## Chapter 4 - Paragons

Paragons represent the second major progression multiplier after creature tiers. While tiers determine the overall strength class of a creature, Paragon Levels continuously enhance its base statistics, allowing even familiar creatures to remain competitive throughout the entire game.

Unlike equipment upgrades, Paragons permanently improve the creature itself and interact multiplicatively with every other progression system, including Tier bonuses, equipment, Unique Saddles and several ability modifiers.

For this reason, long-term creature investment is generally more valuable than repeatedly replacing tames with higher tier alternatives.

@tip:Stat formula
Because Paragons permanently improve creatures (+100% stats pear each paragon gained), investing in favorite tames becomes increasingly valuable over time.

Players should avoid frequently replacing creatures once substantial Paragon progression has been achieved unless a significantly stronger build becomes available. 
@end

Paragons can be earned in several ways - mainly by feeding your tame a matching Paragon Soul, or by completing Paragon quests, viewable from the Paragon icon in your tame's multi-use wheel. There is no required order to complete them, since applying a Paragon fully recalculates the tame's stats.

Group Variant creatures can consume Paragon Souls belonging to any Variant within their group. Seventh-tier variants are excluded from this system.

This makes Paragon farming considerably more flexible once a creature has been upgraded to a Group Variant.

### Long-Term Paragon Progression
Paragon progression should be viewed as a continuous objective rather than a final upgrade.

Many endgame creatures will accumulate dozens of Paragon Levels through normal gameplay while completing quests, defeating bosses and participating in advanced encounters.

Players should therefore avoid postponing Paragon progression until late game, as steady investment provides substantially better long-term returns.

Beacon Bosses, NPC Chiefs and Omega Max creatures all contribute significantly toward Paragon progression and should be incorporated into regular gameplay whenever possible.

These encounters offer some of the highest concentrations of progression rewards available before the final endgame.

@tip:Farm Paragons
The simplest way to farm Paragons is to complete quests dropped by killing wild dinos
@end
