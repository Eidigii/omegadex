## Chapter 8 - Magic Find & Imbue

### What Magic Find Is
Magic Find (MF) is a loot‑quality multiplier that increases the chance of rolling higher‑tier bonuses, better Charms, and stronger cursed drops.

It does not affect damage, combat scaling, or saddle/medallion performance.

Magic Find is purely a loot progression stat, and it becomes essential only in mid‑late and endgame.


### How Magic Find Rolls Work
Magic Find rolls differently depending on item type, imbue count, essence tier, and whether the item is cursed.

MF per single bonus
-Gear: 3% → 9% MF per bonus (Ascendant quality)
-Charms: 12% → 36% MF per bonus (Ascendant quality)

MF can roll multiple times on the same item, but this is rare.

Bonus count depends on essence tier

Higher essence tiers = more bonuses rolled
----
Example: 

Ultimate Essence (Tier 9) → always rolls 9 bonuses (roll 6 bonuses at Elite at Ascendant quality)

More bonuses = higher chance of rolling MF twice or more

### Magic Find Roll Ranges (Charms)

Charms upgraded to Ascendant and imbued with Elite Essence roll MF in the following ranges:

-Common: 12% – 36%
-Rare: 37% – 72%
-Very rare: 73% – 108%

These values apply per MF bonus, meaning multiple MF rolls can stack.

Average Fragment Requirements

600 fragments → usually not enough to exceed 36% MF

1500–3000 fragments → realistic range for rolling 50%+ MF on Charms

### Magic Find Roll Ranges (Gear)
Gear rolls MF much lower:

3% → 9% MF per bonus

Multiple MF rolls are possible but extremely rare

The number of bonuses depends on the essence tier used during imbuing

Gear MF is useful early, but endgame MF progression is Charm‑based.

### Magic Find on Cursed Boss Drops (Charms)
Omega‑tier cursed bosses can drop Charms with massive MF values due to curse multipliers.

Typical Drop Ranges
-Up to 252% MF → fairly common
-Up to 468% MF → significantly rarer
-Up to 648% MF → extremely rare

These values are only possible from cursed boss drops, not from self‑cursing.

A Charm that required three MF imbues to reach its value is often referred to as: Tier 3 MF Charm

### Species Shard Influence on MF Rolls
Magic Find rolls are influenced by the species shard (See Paragon shards) value of the creature associated with the Charm.

Low‑Shard Species (e.g., Dodo) usually tend to be high roll on Ascendant Affinity or Charm, but can still roll maximum values due to random scaling

This system ensures:
-Weak species → more consistent MF rolls
-Strong species → higher potential but less consistency

### Recommended Magic Find Progression Path

This is the optimal MF progression route used by high‑end players:
-35%+ MF → Double Curse → 70%+ MF
-50%+ MF → Double Curse → 100%+ MF
-50%+ MF → Multiply Curse → 150–200%+ MF
-Farm cursed bosses → 300%+, 400%+, 600%+ MF Charms

Multiply Curse is 30× more expensive and fails often, but it can produce 200%+ MF Charms.
