## Chapter 3 - NPC Tribes

NPC Tribes remain one of the most reliable sources of equipment progression throughout the game.

While early encounters primarily reward armor pieces and Unique weapons, later encounters become the primary source of Legendary equipment, cursed gear and advanced crafting materials.

Repeatedly defeating Tribe Leaders and Chiefs should therefore be considered part of the normal progression rather than optional content.

| # | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | NPCs always spawn at loot chests, maximizing spawn diversity without competing with dino spawn points. |
| 2 | Basic through Elite tier NPCs spawn naturally. Higher tiers require points earned by killing Leaders. |
| 3 | NPCs spawn in groups of five: four regular fighters and one Leader. At Omega tier the Leader is replaced by a Chief. |
| 4 | Every NPC wears a full armor set. Leaders carry Unique weapons; Chiefs carry Legendary weapons. |
| 5 | Set pieces and Unique weapons have a high drop chance from their respective NPCs. |
| 6 | A Chief always drops a cursed set and a max-stat Legendary weapon, and resets that tribe's point total. |
----
@tip:Hunting Chiefs
Chiefs are the best single source of cursed set pieces and max-stat Legendary weapons - and defeating one resets that tribe's point progress, effectively resetting the climb toward the next Chief spawn.
@end
