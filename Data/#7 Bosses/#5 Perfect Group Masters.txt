Perfect Group Masters:
An upgrade from the Group Master, these are almost identical with a couple of differences. First, they have the
Corrupt (7th) variant from their group. The Perfect Elemental Group Master will also have Barrage. They can
also only be damaged by something from the same group, as the intention is to kill them with something that is
capable of using the soul they drop.

Killing a Perfect Group Master will drop a Perfect Group Master Soul on the ground. The Perfect Group Master
Soul can be used to upgrade your tame's variant to the Group Variant. Fire will be upgraded to Elemental, Comet
to Cosmic, etc. While this can be fed to corrupt variants, they will lose that ability.
