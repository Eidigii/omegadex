Corrupt Masters:
Corrupt Masters are centered around the Corrupt variants (also known as 7th variants). They have 10x the
stats of Masters, and have the Corrupt variant from their group. A Fire Corrupt Master will also have the
Barrage variant, etc. A Barrage Corrupt Master will get a random variant from it's associated group. They have
10x the stats of a Master, and are summoned by placing a Master Soul back into the beacon.

You have choices from here.
1. Feed the Soul to an Apex of the same variant to upgrade it to Ultimate Tier.
2. Place the Corrupt Master soul into the Altar with 5 other Corrupt Master souls of the same group to summon
a Group Master.
