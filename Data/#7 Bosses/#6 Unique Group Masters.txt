Unique Group Masters:
Unique Gropu Masters take things even further than Group Masters. They have Group Variants, but 2 of them,
and 10x the base stats of a Group master. To Summon one, put the 2 matching Group Masters Souls in the
Omega Altar. Be warned, Unique Group Masters have 12 variant abilities and the fight can be visually intense.

Current Unique Group Masters:
-Apocalypse: Cosmic/Nature
-Anima: Mythical/Summoner
-Alchemy: Unstable/Elemental
-Indestructus: Guardian/Resource
-Infinity: Ethereal/Utility
-Sin: Nightmare/Rage

You have choices from here.
1. Feed the Soul to an Ancient of a variant within the groups of the Unique Group Master killed. This will rank it
up to Endless, doubling its stats.
2. Place the Unique Group Master soul into the Omega Beacon to summon an Aspect.
3. Place the Unique Group Master soul into the Altar with the 2 matching 7th variant Master souls from the
same groups to summon the Perfect Unique Group Master.
4. Place all 6 Unique Group Master Souls into the Omega Altar to summon Zodiac, the final boss in normal Tier
progression.
