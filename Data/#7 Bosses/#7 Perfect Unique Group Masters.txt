Perfect Unique Group Masters:
Perfect Unique Group Masters have both of the matching corrupt (7th) variants of their respective groups.
While they have the same stats as the Unique Group Master, they can only be damaged by tames of their
respective groups, as the intention is to kill them with something that is capable of using the soul they drop.

Feeding the Perfect Unique Group Master Soul to a group variant tame will upgrade it to a Unique Group
Variant.
