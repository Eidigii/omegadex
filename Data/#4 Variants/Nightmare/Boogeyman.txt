Boogeyman: Ruler of the night, the Boogeyman is an absolute force to be reckoned with. A 
fairly normal creature during the day, at night they become covered in a solid black cloak, 
unable to be targeted by other creatures. At night, they also gain huge damage reduction, 
night vision, and the ability to jump-scare enemies with a powerful roar.

Pressing Prone(X) will make the Boogeyman release a terrifying Roar. This will do huge
damage to the enemies, as if scaring the life out of them. It will also force them to flee for
an extended period.
